## Full Description
Tayyib: Halal Food Scanner is an independent mobile application created to help Muslims worldwide make more informed everyday food choices—without requiring an account, login, or subscription.

Simply point your camera at a packaged food barcode, scan an ingredient list with the built-in Lens OCR scanner, pick a packaging photo, search by product or brand, or paste ingredients offline to inspect E-numbers, packaging claims, and available Halal assessments.

### Key Features
- **Fast On-Device Barcode Scanner**: Supports EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E, Code 128, Code 39, and QR product codes with flashlight support, manual barcode entry, haptic feedback, and distinct audio chimes for Halal, Haram, Doubtful, and Unknown results.
- **Built-In Lens Label Scanner (On-Device OCR)**: Point your camera at any ingredient list or choose a photo using Android's zero-permission Photo Picker to read and analyze ingredients completely offline, plus an optional shortcut to open Google Lens for non-Latin labels.
- **Reliable Dual-Source Product Lookup**: Queries Halal Open Food Facts first and automatically falls back to Open Food Facts (`world.openfoodfacts.org`) if the primary service is unavailable, with local Room offline caching for previously viewed items.
- **Transparent 4-Pillar Assessment**: Clearly separates community evaluations, Tayyib's local ingredient & E-number analysis, packaging certification metadata, and evidence quality—never presenting guesswork as certainty.
- **Inline Halal Alternatives**: Whenever a product is flagged Haram or Doubtful, view real suggested Halal alternatives directly on the product page or browse live Halal products by category on the Home and Alternatives tabs.
- **Offline E-Number & Ingredient Guide**: Built-in bilingual dictionary covering key food additives (including E120, E441, E471, E472, E481, E482, E476, E322, E904, E542, and more) with typical origins, alternative origins, and scholarly context.
- **Worldwide & Bilingual (English / French)**: Switch languages instantly in-app with the EN | FR toggle without restarting.
- **Privacy-First & Account-Free**: Scan history, saved favourites, and product cache stay locally on your device. No account registration, no ads, and no tracking SDKs.

### Independence & Religious Notice
Tayyib is an independent application developed by Muzamil Ahmad and is not an official app of Halal Open Food Facts, Open Food Facts, or any certification authority. Product recipes can change without notice—always verify the physical packaging and consult trusted certification bodies or qualified scholars when uncertain.
